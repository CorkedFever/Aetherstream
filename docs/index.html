A TELEVISION IN EORZEA

LIVE FROM THE LIFESTREAM

Aetherstream is a Dalamud plugin that puts a working screen in Final Fantasy XIV — painted onto a real furnishing, so the game lights and occludes the picture like anything else in the room. Tune in live TV, Twitch, YouTube, your Plex library or the Internet Archive; leave it on a station of its own, twenty-eight channels drawn from the game's data with hosts, schedules and commercial breaks; and watch together from a six-character party code.

INSTALL SOURCE

WHAT'S ON

CH 1

Live TV

Thousands of channels from the public iptv-org directory, or any M3U playlist of your own — a channel guide, numbered presets, channel up/down. A channel whose address moves is found again on its own.

CH 2

Your libraries

Plex, browsed as posters. YouTube: search, playlists, and your own feeds. Twitch: who you follow that is live, the top, a game. Pluto TV's free films and series. Red Bull TV's documentaries. PBS. NASA+. TED. Dailymotion. The Internet Archive's public domain films. Videos on your own machine.

CH 3

The station

Twenty-eight channels the set draws for itself from the game's data: a weather forecast with a pixie, a cooking show with a Namazu, a wildlife show with a moogle, a tonberry telling the tales of the Twelve, a shopping channel with a goblin, a film channel, and commercial breaks between.

CH 4

Music

Under the channels: the bundled lounge tracks, your folder, a Plex playlist, the game's own orchestrion rolls, thirty thousand internet radio stations, or a podcast. The Radio channel shows what is on.

CH 5

Watch together

Make a party, send the six-character code. Everyone who pastes it sees your broadcast on their own screen, in their own house.

CH 6

Real data, silly hosts

The prices are the real market board, the recipes are your crafting log, the weather is the game's, the vistas are the Sightseeing Log's own paintings. The hosts are moogles, tonberries, goblins and kobolds, and every word they say is written.

INSTALL

Aetherstream is installed through Dalamud's plugin installer, from a custom repository. Two minutes, no downloads by hand.

Add the repository

In the game, open Dalamud settings (/xlsettings) → Experimental → Custom Plugin Repositories. Paste this address, press the +, and save:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CorkedFever/Aetherstream/main/repo.json

Install the plugin

Open the plugin installer (/xlplugins), search for Aetherstream, and install it.

Turn it on

/aether opens the set. /aether play <source> starts something straight away; /aether stop ends it.

WHAT IT NEEDS

The plugin ships with its own decoder — most of it works with nothing installed. A couple of sources want a helper on your machine, and Setup, behind the gear on the remote, shows at a glance what has been found.

FFXIV with Dalamudrequired

Launch the game through XIVLauncher. If you already use plugins, you have this.
Nothing elseTwitch · Plex · Live TV · direct URLs

These work out of the box.
yt-dlpYouTube, Kick, most other sites

In PowerShell: winget install --id yt-dlp.yt-dlp --exact — then restart the game; it reads PATH at startup. Already downloaded it somewhere? Point Setup's file picker at it instead.
DenoYouTube specifically

winget install --id DenoLand.Deno --exact, then restart the game. yt-dlp uses it to solve YouTube's challenges — without it, YouTube half-works at best.
A signed-in browseryour YouTube and Twitch feeds

Your recommended, subscriptions and follows come from a browser you are signed in to, chosen under Setup → Sources. Firefox and its forks (Floorp, LibreWolf) work directly; Chrome, Edge and Brave need a cookies file exported from the browser.
ffmpegonly to broadcast to a party

winget install --id Gyan.FFmpeg --exact. Watching a party needs nothing.

HOW TO USE IT

Put the picture on a screen

Setup → Screen → Known screens → Everkeep Monitor. Stand next to your monitor furnishing and click — the picture lands on the nearest one, fitted and ready. One tip worth the trouble: recolour the wall behind it black in-game. The panel is an additive effect, and a black wall is the single biggest picture-quality win.

No furnishing? A floating panel works anywhere — it just draws over the world instead of living in it.

Tune something in

The window is a remote beside a television. Press Home on the remote for the apps: Plex, Jellyfin or Emby, YouTube, Twitch, Dailymotion, Pluto, Red Bull, PBS, NASA, TED, the Archive, Local videos, and Paste a link, which takes anything — a Twitch channel name, a YouTube link, a direct stream URL, or a party code. Fold the window and only the remote stays. The Live TV app is a channel guide — search it, then right-click a channel to pin it. Pins become numbered presets, and the remote's channel ▲/▼ and last-channel buttons step through them like a real set.

Run your own station? Add an ErsatzTV or Tunarr playlist alongside the public list from the guide's playlist picker.

Leave it on a channel

The Aetherstream app is the station: Info for what is on, what is up and what it costs; Shows for the programmes with hosts, on a clock so everyone watching sees the same episode; Ambience for the aquarium, the fireplace and the screensavers. The Music app picks what plays under them, with Radio, Podcasts and Orchestrion apps beside it.

Watch together

In the Watch party app — already signed in to the Aetherstream party server, nothing to enter — make a party and copy its six-character code. Friends paste the code into their Paste a link app — that's the whole of it, once, forever. Whatever you broadcast, the room sees on their own screens.

Your invite can carry your screen setup too, so guests land the picture on the same furnishing in one click.

READING THE SET

The screen tells you what's happening the way a television would — no logs to dig through.

NO SIGNAL

Nothing is playing. Pick an input below the screen.

TUNING…

Connecting and buffering. Give it a few seconds.

SIGNAL LOST

The stream stopped sending. Press ↻ to pick up where it stopped — live TV channels retry themselves.

NO PICTURE

Something's wrong, and the reason is written right under the words.
